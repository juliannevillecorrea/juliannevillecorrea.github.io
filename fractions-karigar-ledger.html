LEDGER NOTE, PAGE 1

Meera runs a small tailoring stall near the market. One morning a customer wants a kurta that needs 2⅓ metres of cloth, but Meera has only a 5¾ metre roll left after the last order. Before she can quote a price or promise a delivery date, she has to know: how much cloth will be left over, and is it enough for the matching dupatta? That question — asked in a hundred different forms by tailors, carpenters, electricians and farmers across the country's Skill India training centres every single day — is exactly what this page will get you fluent in.
